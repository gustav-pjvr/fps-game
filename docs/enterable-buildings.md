# Enterable Valley buildings (v4.6, section 9 of docs/squad-arsenal-night.md)

## Part 1, as built (branch feature/houses)

- **Shared door module** (`world/doors/`): `doorGeometry.ts` (pure: `DoorPlane` with an optional floor `y`, pick box, ray test, walk crossing, body in doorway, distance), `SlidingDoorView.ts` (two steel leaves, status lamps, a collider enabled only while shut) and `interactiveObjects.ts` (`InteractiveWorldObjects`, the world side `core/facilityInteraction.ts` drives). The Facility's `FacilityObjects` uses them unchanged in behaviour; `facilityDoors.ts` re-exports the moved helpers.
- **Layout** (`world/houses/houseLayout.ts`, pure, tested): per design its walls with real openings (one front door, windows, inner doorways), floors, ceilings, rooms with a ceiling lamp each, furniture (crates, tables, shelves, cots) and on the block a stair (a ramp slab collider under drawn steps, 32 degrees) to the upper floor with guard rails. `wallPieces` cuts a wall round its openings; `doorToWorld` and `houseToWorld` place things for a house turned in quarter turns.
  - Bunkhouse: front room and bunk room through a doorway. Its floor is now level with the street (the plinth is the walls' lowest concrete course), because the player controller does not step a 0.3 m sill reliably (found by the walk test). The small front window stays a glazed panel: the door leaves slide into the wall behind it.
  - Block: ground floor and upper floor; the upper front window looks over the street.
  - Workshop: hall and a back office through a doorway; the roller door stays shut; its windows are lower (1.5 to 2.25 m) so they can be seen and shot through.
- **Designs** (`world/houseDesigns.ts`): walls are colliders drawn as an outer skin and an interior lining (`inner-*` materials in `structureKit.ts`: a flat AO map scales only indirect light, so rooms are dim by day while sun through a window and lamps light them fully). `build()` is the shell, `buildInterior()` the furniture, partitions, stair, rails and lamp housings, hidden past 70 m (`ValleyHouses.INTERIOR_DRAW_DISTANCE`).
- **Doors** (`world/houses/ValleyDoors.ts`): start closed; interact within 2.2 m while looking at one; refuses to close on a player (the interaction's existing rule). State is the set of open door ids, kept while a house streams out. Ids (`valleyHouseIds.ts`): house key from (town cell gx, gz, index in the town layout), times 4, plus slot (door 0; lamps use the room index). Multiplayer reuses `worldInteract` unchanged and `worldObjectState` / the welcome's `worldObjects` with a new optional `valleyDoors: number[]` (empty `doors`/`breakers` strings on the Valley). The host checks a guest's door against `BuildingManager.houseAt`, so it works for houses the host has not streamed in.
- **Lamps** (`world/houses/HouseLights.ts`): one instanced emissive panel per room (the Facility's USE_COLOR tint pattern), lit at Night and in Fog; 4 point lights created at match start and moved each frame to the lit lamps nearest the camera (Day creates none). Shooting one breaks it locally (`Game.handleHitscanRay`, sparks and sound); dead lamps stay dead through streaming.
- **Spawns**: `BuildingManager.isPointInsideBuilding` already tests each design's full footprint (eaves included) plus the clearance, which contains every interior, and enemy position picks use the same check. No pickup placement exists on this branch.
- **Testing aids**: `?weather=night` (or fog) gives a solo match that weather (`core/devWeather.ts`). Debug API: `getValleyHouses()`, `setValleyDoor(id, open)`, `getHouseLights()`.

## Part 2 must do

- **Enemies and doors**: on the Valley call `setEnemyDoorGate(interaction.enemyGate)` (Game.ts, the Valley branch next to the Facility one); `ValleyDoors.closedDoorAhead` is ready. A walk grid per house from `HOUSE_LAYOUTS` (rooms, `wallPieces`, furniture, `layout.door`, `stair`), and an enemy whose target is inside goes to the door first. Closed doors should block enemy sight (their collider already blocks shots).
- **Cover points inside**: from `layout.furniture` and wall corners, in world space with `houseToWorld`; register with the `ObstacleRegistry` in `BuildingManager.placeForChunk`.
- **Minimap interiors once entered**: rooms, walls and openings from the layout; door state from `ValleyDoors.state`.
- **Lamp sync**: `HouseLights.killLamp(id, effect)`, `deadLampIds()`, `isDead(id)`; ids are `houseObjectId(houseKey(ref), roomIndex)`. Send on break (like `lampDestroyed`) and in the welcome, and apply `killLamp(id, false)` for late joiners.
- **Browser test**: an e2e spec using the debug hooks above (find a house, open its door, walk in, shoot a lamp, a guest sees the door state).
- Open: a frame-rate reading on real hardware (the headless run's numbers were taken right after teleports, while chunks streamed and shaders compiled).
